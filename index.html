Tandoor Corner

[tun-DOOR · KOR-ner] noun • very Kolhapuri

A place on the Radhanagari road where you stop for “just one quick plate”, then order a tandoori, then another roti, then one more bowl of rassa, and end up staying far longer than planned. Known to cause sudden hunger, loud cries of “ajun ek vati rassa!” (one more bowl!) and family lunches that never finish on time.

Synonyms: delicious trap, the family table, sudden hunger
